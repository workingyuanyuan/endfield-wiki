# q13: When did Camille's banner run?

Camille's banner, "Expunger of Sin" (Chartered Headhunting, Camille rate-up), opened 2026-06-26 12:00 and ended at the Version 1.4 update (2026-07-16). Times are Asia server time (UTC+8).

Conditions:
- Dates and times are Asia server time (UTC+8). The Americas / Europe server has update maintenance at the same moment, 17:00 to 23:00 (UTC-5) the day before; timed content ends at the same clock time in each server's own time.
- The banner opened after the previous banner, Fists of No Regrets (Mi Fu), which ended 2026-06-26 11:59.
- Camille is a 6-star operator who arrived with Version 1.3 (update 2026-06-05); her banner opened 3 weeks later than the update.
- The paired weapon banner (Crimson Hued Issue, Blessing of Lustrous Carmine) opened 2026-06-26 12:00.
- After the banner, Camille stays in the Chartered pool without rate-up for two more Chartered banners.

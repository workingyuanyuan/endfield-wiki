## q18
6-star weapons come mainly from Arsenal Issues (weapon gacha), paid with Arsenal Tickets (1980 per attempt, or one Issue Cert; tickets come from operators pulled in headhunting: 2000 per 6-star operator, 200 per 5-star, 20 per 4-star). One attempt gives 10 weapons with at least one 5-star or better; 6-star rate 4%, 25% of 6-stars are the rate-up weapon; 6-star guaranteed on 4th attempt after 3 without. They can also be bought in the Arsenal Exchange for 2480 Arsenal Tickets (one 6-star slot, weekly). Duration: a regular Arsenal Issue opens with a Chartered banner and closes with the third Chartered banner counted from it, so about 7 to 13 weeks for those that closed (several open at once). RE-Factor Issues open and close with their RE-Factor banner; Fest of Brilliance Issues closed with it.
URLs: /systems/ , /systems/arsenal/

## q22

## q27
Damage is a product of multipliers: base damage (usually from ATK), critical, DMG bonus, DMG Reduction, Amp, Weakness, Protect, Susceptible, other damage-taken increases, DEF, Stagger (x1.3), Resistance, Off-field, Link, etc. DEF multiplier = 100 / (DEF + 100) (1 for damage that ignores DEF). Enemies have 100 base DEF at every level, so the multiplier is 0.5 (halves damage) unless an effect changes their DEF. Conditions: formulas are from NGA player tests in Version 1.0; applies to ordinary damage (not Physical Statuses, Arts Reactions, Arts Bursts, which use other level/intensity multipliers); displayed values are rounded down so results may be off a few points.
URL: http://127.0.0.1:4398/endfield-wiki/systems/combat/

## q32

## q24
Answer: Vulnerable is the stack (up to 4) that a first Physical Status hit puts on an enemy; Lift and Knock Down add stacks, Crush and Breach consume them. Susceptible (Susceptibility) is a different thing: a debuff that makes a target take more damage of one element.
Qualifier: Do not confuse them. Vulnerable applies to Physical Status mechanics; Susceptibility (e.g. Arts Susceptibility from Purrchena's ultimate) raises damage taken for a time.
Source: http://127.0.0.1:4398/endfield-wiki/systems/combat/

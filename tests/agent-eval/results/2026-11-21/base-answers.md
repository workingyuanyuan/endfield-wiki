## q15
Answer: Base 6-star rate 0.8%. Soft pity: after 65 pulls without a 6-star, each pull adds +5% (5.8% on pull 66...); hard pity at pull 80. 5-star guarantee every 10 pulls. Rate-up: 50% chance a 6-star is the rate-up operator; once per banner, the 120th pull is guaranteed the rate-up operator; every 240th pull gives a Potential token.
Qualifier: The 80-pull 6-star counter carries over across banners of the same type until a 6-star appears. The 120-pull rate-up guarantee does NOT carry over and resets when the banner ends (in RE-Factor banners it can trigger only once across banners of the same name). The 240 counter also resets. Ended limited operators join the next two Chartered banners' 6-star roster ("lose the 50/50"). Headhunting Dossier (after 60 pulls) is consumed on the next banner's first pull as a free 10-pull.
Source: https://endfield.wiki.gg/wiki/Headhunting

## q24
Answer: Vulnerable is a debuff stacking up to four times, applied when an operator tries to apply a Physical Status to a not-yet-Vulnerable enemy; applying it to a Solidified enemy consumes Solidification and triggers Shatter (240 to 600% ATK Physical DMG). Susceptibility is a category of per-element debuffs (Physical, Arts, Heat, Electric, Cryo, Nature) making targets take more DMG of that element; effects of the same type stack.
Qualifier: As of October 2026 no operator directly applies Susceptibility, though Ardelia's Dolly Rush applies Physical and Arts Susceptibility. Arts Susceptible also counts as Susceptible to all Arts elements.
Source: https://endfield.wiki.gg/wiki/Susceptibility

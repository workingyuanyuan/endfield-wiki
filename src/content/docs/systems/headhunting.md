## Rates and pity

Headhunting is how operators are obtained. Every pull has a 0.8% base rate for a 6-star operator, and a 6-star is guaranteed within 80 counted pulls; Urgent Recruitment pulls do not count. A Chartered Headhunting banner features one rate-up 6-star operator, guaranteed within the first 120 pulls on that banner; that count does not move to the next banner, but the 80-pull count does. A rate-up operator stays in the Chartered pool for three Chartered banners, its own included, and is never added to Basic Headhunting. Up to Version 1.6, two former rate-up operators came back in RE-Factor Headhunting #1, Yvonne in Version 1.5 and Tangtang in Version 1.6; its 120-pull rate-up guarantee counts once across all banners of that name, and the count carries over between them. Laevatain and Gilberta came back only in the Special banner Fest of Brilliance.

## Banner types

| Type | Runs | What it offers | Pulls with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Headhunting | Permanent | The basic roster, no rate-up | Basic HH Permit |
| Chartered Headhunting (rate-up banner) | One banner at a time, about 16 to 35 days each from Scars of the Forge to Rejuvenation | One rate-up 6-star operator; eight 6-star operators in the pool | The banner's own HH Permit, Chartered HH Permit, Oroberyl |
| RE-Factor Headhunting (rerun banner) | Numbered by name (#1, #2); since Version 1.5 | A former rate-up operator again; six 6-star operators in the pool | Chartered HH Permit, Oroberyl |
| Special Headhunting | Fest of Brilliance, Version 1.2 | No rate-up; four 6-star operators and a free pick of one of them at 120 pulls | Shimmering Moment HH Permit, Chartered HH Permit, Oroberyl |
| New Horizons Headhunting | Each new account, until its 40 pulls are spent and the 6-star weapon is chosen | One 6-star operator of the basic roster and a 6-star weapon selector within 40 pulls | Permits from beginner milestones |

Chartered, RE-Factor and Special Headhunting open to an account once it has completed the main mission Chapter I Process I: Break the Siege. The Chartered HH Permit works in RE-Factor Headhunting since Version 1.5. Each account that completes the main mission Chapter I Process I: Break the Siege gets a Designation Selection Permit, which picks one 6-star operator of Basic Headhunting; it was first sent after the quick update of 2026-02-05. The permanent event Beginner's Chartered Tribute, added in Version 1.1, gives Chartered HH Permit ×10 for progress in Chapter I.

## Rates and guarantees (pity)

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Base rates | 6-star 0.8%, 5-star 8.0%, 4-star 91.2% |
| 5-star or better | Guaranteed within 10 pulls |
| Any 6-star (hard pity) | Guaranteed within 80 pulls |
| Rising 6-star rate (soft pity) | After 65 pulls without a 6-star, +5 points per pull: 5.8% on pull 66, 10.8% on pull 67 |
| Rate-up share (50/50) | An ordinary 6-star from Chartered Headhunting is the rate-up operator 50% of the time |
| Rate-up operator | Guaranteed within the first 120 pulls: once on each Chartered banner; once across all RE-Factor banners of one name |
| Token | Every 240 pulls, one Token of the rate-up operator |

The 10-pull and 80-pull guarantees and the rising rate hold on Basic, Chartered and RE-Factor Headhunting; the 80-pull count is kept per type. Basic Headhunting has no rate-up. Special Headhunting (Fest of Brilliance) counted its 10-pull and 80-pull guarantees alone.

## How often a 6-star is not the rate-up operator

An ordinary 6-star from a Chartered banner is the rate-up operator half the time. The other half it is one of the seven other 6-star operators of the pool: the two other limited operators in that pool and the five standard ones. A specific earlier rate-up operator can therefore come out of a Chartered banner only on such a loss, and only while it is still in the pool. If the first 119 counted pulls on a banner give no rate-up operator, pull 120 gives it. This guarantee applies once per banner and does not limit later losses.

## What carries over to the next banner

The 120-pull rate-up guarantee does not carry over from one Chartered banner to the next: it resets when the banner ends. The 80-pull 6-star count carries over to the next banner of the same type, until a 6-star appears.

| Count or reward | Chartered Headhunting | RE-Factor Headhunting | Special (Fest of Brilliance) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Any 6-star (80) | Carries over to the next Chartered banner until a 6-star appears | Shared by all RE-Factor banners, whatever the name; carries over until a 6-star appears | Counted alone; nothing carries in or out |
| Rate-up operator (120) | First 120 pulls of each banner; does not carry over | Once across all banners of one name; the count carries over to them | No rate-up; 120 pulls give a Shimmering Moment Designation Permit, once |
| Urgent Recruitment | One free 10× after 30 pulls; lost when the banner ends | One free 10× each after 30, 60 and 90 pulls, once across one name; unused ones carry over to it | One free 10× after 30 pulls; lost when the banner ends |
| 60 pulls | Headhunting Dossier, used on the next Chartered banner | The second of the three Urgent Recruitments | Basic HH Permit ×10, once |
| 240 pulls | Token of the rate-up operator; counted per banner | Token of the rate-up operator; the count carries over to the same name | Shimmering Moment Token Supply: a Token of one of the four, for an operator you own |
| Pulls counted for rewards | Per banner | Carry over to banners of the same name | Reset to zero when the banner ends |

## RE-Factor (rerun) and Chartered (rate-up) banners: what differs

- Pool: a RE-Factor pool holds six 6-star operators, the rerun operator and the five standard ones; a Chartered pool holds eight.
- 120-pull guarantee: on a Chartered banner it counts per banner and resets; on RE-Factor banners it counts once across all banners of one name, and pulls carry over to the next banner of that name (a later rerun of the same name counts as #2 and keeps the counts of #1).
- Urgent Recruitment: a Chartered banner gives one free 10× at 30 pulls; RE-Factor banners of one name give three, at 30, 60 and 90 pulls, and unused ones carry over.
- Headhunting Dossier: only Chartered banners give it.
- Permits: the Chartered HH Permit and Oroberyl work on both; Chartered banners also accept their own HH Permit.
- Effect on Chartered progress: none. The 80-pull count is kept per type: pulls on RE-Factor banners do not move the Chartered count, and pulls on Chartered banners do not move the RE-Factor count.

## Rerun weapon banners (RE-Factor Issues)

Each RE-Factor Headhunting banner runs with a RE-Factor Issue, the weapon banner of the same rerun, which opens and closes with it. Both RE-Factor Issues brought back the seven 6-star weapons of the original Arsenal Issue, the rate-up weapon among them. Its rules differ from a regular Arsenal Issue in what carries over:

| Rule | Arsenal Issue | RE-Factor Issue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Any 6-star weapon on the 4th attempt after 3 without one | Yes | Yes; counts only in the current banner, not in a later rerun of the same name |
| Rate-up weapon on the 8th attempt if the first 7 gave none | Once per Issue; resets when it ends | Once across all banners of one name; the count carries over |
| Attempt rewards | Count resets when the Issue ends | Count carries over to the same name |



## Milestone rewards

- Urgent Recruitment: ten free pulls at the banner's base rates, with a 5-star or better guaranteed. They count neither toward any guarantee nor toward the pull totals for rewards, so a rate-up operator won in them leaves the 120-pull guarantee untouched.
- Headhunting Dossier: sent to the Valuables Stash after 60 pulls on a Chartered banner. It gives ten pulls on the next Chartered banner, which are lost when that banner ends. These pulls count toward the guarantees and the rising rate.
- Token Tribute: every 240 pulls on one Chartered banner give a Token (a duplicate for Potential) of its rate-up operator.

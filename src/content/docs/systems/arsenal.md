6-star weapons come mainly from Arsenal Issues, the weapon gacha, paid with Arsenal Tickets that every operator pulled in headhunting brings: 2000 for a 6-star, 200 for a 5-star, 20 for a 4-star. One Issue attempt gives 10 weapons with at least one 5-star or better. Each Arsenal Issue has one rate-up 6-star weapon and stays open for three Chartered Headhunting banners, counted from the one it opens with, so several Issues are open at once; RE-Factor Issues and the two Issues that opened with Fest of Brilliance had an end date of their own. Essences, dropped by enemies, raise the level of a weapon's skills.

## Where Arsenal Tickets come from

| Source | What it gives |
| --- | --- |
| Operators from headhunting (Chartered, RE-Factor, Special) | 2000 per 6-star, 200 per 5-star, 20 per 4-star |
| Operators from events, purchases and selectors | The same amounts |
| Weekly Routine (permanent, in the Event Center) | Arsenal Tickets among weekly mission rewards |
| Origeometry | Exchanged for Arsenal Tickets |

When Arsenal Tickets run short during an Issue, Origeometry can be spent instead; since Version 1.2 the prompt for this can be turned off for the rest of the day.

## Arsenal Exchange: buying weapons outright

Arsenal Tickets also buy weapons directly in the Arsenal Exchange (Acquisition Center), whose stock changes from time to time:

| Weapon rarity | Price in Arsenal Tickets | Rotation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6-star | 2480 | One slot, weekly |
| 5-star | 400 | One slot weekly, two slots daily, and a permanent list |
| 4-star | 20 | One slot daily, and a permanent list |

## What an Issue attempt costs and gives

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Cost | 1980 Arsenal Tickets or one Issue Cert per attempt |
| Weapons per attempt | 10, at least one 5-star or better |
| Rates | 6-star 4%, 5-star 15%, 4-star 81% |
| Rate-up share | 25% of 6-star weapons are the rate-up weapon |
| Pool | Seven 6-star weapons, the rate-up one included |
| AIC Quota | 10 with each 5-star and 50 with each 6-star weapon from an Issue |

## Guarantees and what carries over

| Rule | Arsenal Issue | RE-Factor Issue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5-star or better | In every attempt | In every attempt |
| Any 6-star weapon | On the 4th attempt after 3 without one | On the 4th attempt after 3 without one; counts only in the current banner, not even in a later rerun of the same name |
| Rate-up 6-star weapon | On the 8th attempt if the first 7 gave none, once per Issue; resets when it ends | On the 8th attempt if the first 7 gave none, once across all banners of one name; the count carries over to them |
| Attempt rewards | Arms Offering Crate at 10 attempts, the rate-up weapon at 18, then one of the two in turn every 8; the count resets when the Issue ends | The same steps; the count carries over to the same name |

The Arms Offering Crate is a weapon selection box.

## How long an Arsenal Issue stays

A regular Arsenal Issue opens with a Chartered Headhunting banner and closes with the third Chartered banner counted from it, so three run side by side for most of the time. Those that have closed stayed about 7 to 13 weeks, because Chartered banners vary in length. A RE-Factor Issue opens and closes with its RE-Factor Headhunting banner, and the two Issues opened with Fest of Brilliance closed with it. A permanent Issue also runs.

## RE-Factor Issues

RE-Factor Issues began in Version 1.5. Each brought back an older Issue's rate-up weapon with the same seven 6-star weapons: Tag Artist Issue repeats the pool of Graffiti Issue, and Tender Sprig Issue that of Budding Anew Issue. Each ran beside the RE-Factor Headhunting of the operator whose Chartered banner had opened with the original Issue: Yvonne (Resplendent Spectrum) and Tangtang (Ancestral Spring Flows Anew).

## Smelting Fire and Swift Walker Issues

Smelting Fire Issue and Swift Walker Issue opened on 2026-05-14 with Fest of Brilliance as Issues of their own. They share their rate-up weapons with Smelting Forge Issue and Express Delivery Issue, but their pools of 6-star weapons differ, and so do their Traditional Chinese names (熔灼申領 against 熔鑄申領, 疾行申領 against 迅行申領). Arms Offering Crates exist under both names. The English fan wiki instead describes the two names as English renames of the first two Issues, made in Version 1.2 to fix a mistranslation.

## Essences

Essences are items that raise the level of weapon skills. Each Essence carries up to three stats; infused into a weapon, it raises the level of each weapon skill that matches one of its stats. The effect can be previewed before infusing. An Essence sits in one weapon at a time and can be taken out and moved to another weapon at any time. A weapon whose three skills all match its Essence gains a small glowing part.

| Essence | Traditional Chinese | Rarity | Stats | Levels added | Where it drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flawless Essence | 無瑕基質 | 5 | 3 | Up to +6 for attribute and secondary stats, up to +3 for skill stats, through Essence Etching | Rewards of a Severe Energy Alluvium, claimed with Sanity; unlocked at Exploration Level 4 |
| Pure Essence | 高純基質 | 4 | 3 | Up to +3 | Higher-level enemies |
| Clean Essence | 淨態基質 | 3 | 2 | Up to +2 | Lower-level enemies |
| Stable Essence | 穩定基質 | 2 | 1 | +1 | Lower-level enemies |

Turbid Essence (渾濁基質, rarity 1) is not obtainable in the game.

Storage and disassembly:

- At most 2,000 Essences can be held. At the limit, Essences on the ground can no longer be picked up, and Essences claimed from a Severe Energy Alluvium go to the lost-and-found cache instead.
- Stable, Clean and Pure Essences can be disassembled for Stock Bills of the region where they were obtained: 20, 50 and 100.
- Flawless Essences cannot be disassembled. They are used in Essence Etching.

## Essence Etching and Coolant Gel

Essence Etching raises one stat of a Flawless Essence. The player picks the stat and a second Flawless Essence to consume. On success the stat gains a level. On failure the target Essence stays as it was and the player receives Coolant Gel ×10.

Coolant Gel (冷卻脂) guarantees an etch: spent instead of a second Flawless Essence, it raises the chosen stat for certain. The chance of success falls as the stat's level rises; the menu shows it as Very High, High, Low or Very Low. The Coolant Gel cost rises with it:

| Stat kind | Level reached | Success chance | Coolant Gel for a sure etch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attribute and secondary stats | 2 | Very High | 30 |
| Attribute and secondary stats | 3 | High | 60 |
| Attribute and secondary stats | 4 | Low | 120 |
| Attribute and secondary stats | 5 | Very Low | 250 |
| Attribute and secondary stats | 6 | Very Low | 450 |
| Skill stats | 2 | Low | 120 |
| Skill stats | 3 | Very Low | 300 |

## Levels and promotion

An operator reaches level 90 through four promotions, which raise the level cap to 40, 60, 80 and 90. Promotions I to III take the same materials for all 35 operators; Promotion IV takes Protoset ×36, T-Creds ×100,000, 20 of one rare material and 8 of one fungus, and only those two items change from operator to operator. Rarity changes none of the costs.

## Level caps and promotion materials

| Promotion | Level cap after it | Materials, same for every operator | Gear unlock at this stage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| None | 20 | | |
| Promotion I | 40 | Protodisk ×8, Pink Bolete ×3, T-Creds ×1,600 | Outfitting I: blue quality gear, T-Creds ×1,600 |
| Promotion II | 60 | Protodisk ×25, Red Bolete ×5, T-Creds ×6,500 | Outfitting II: purple quality gear, T-Creds ×6,500 |
| Promotion III | 80 | Protoset ×24, Ruby Bolete ×5, T-Creds ×18,000 | Outfitting III: gold quality gear, T-Creds ×18,000 |
| Promotion IV | 90 | Protoset ×36, a rare material ×20, a fungus ×8, T-Creds ×100,000 | |

All four promotions together cost Protodisk ×33, Protoset ×60 and T-Creds ×126,100; the three Outfitting nodes add T-Creds ×26,100. Promotions II, III and IV each require the previous Outfitting node.

The level-up cost from level 1 is the same for every operator.

| Up to level | Operator EXP | T-Creds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 22,860 | 820 |
| 40 | 271,400 | 13,360 |
| 60 | 747,110 | 37,260 |
| 80 | 1,212,340 | 146,440 |
| 90 | 1,792,290 | 385,420 |

Level 1 to 90 with all four promotions therefore takes 1,792,290 EXP and T-Creds ×511,520. Combat Records give EXP up to level 60 and Cognitive Carriers from level 61 to 90; EXP above a level cap is lost, unlike weapon EXP.

## Skill levels

Each operator has four skills (basic attack, battle skill, combo skill, ultimate), raised one by one in the Combat section of the Ability Matrix. Every skill has 12 levels: ranks 1 to 9 and Mastery 1 to 3. The promotion each level needs and its cost are the same for every skill:

| Skill level | Promotion needed | Cost of the level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | None | Starting level |
| 2 | Promotion I | Protoprism ×6, T-Creds ×1,000 |
| 3 | Promotion I | Protoprism ×12, T-Creds ×2,700 |
| 4 | Promotion II | Protoprism ×16, T-Creds ×3,200 |
| 5 | Promotion II | Protoprism ×21, T-Creds ×4,200 |
| 6 | Promotion II | Protoprism ×27, T-Creds ×5,400 |
| 7 | Promotion III | Protohedron ×6, T-Creds ×8,200 |
| 8 | Promotion III | Protohedron ×8, T-Creds ×10,500 |
| 9 | Promotion III | Protohedron ×15, T-Creds ×18,000 |
| Mastery 1 | Promotion IV | Mark of Perseverance ×1, Protohedron ×15, T-Creds ×24,000 |
| Mastery 2 | Promotion IV | Mark of Perseverance ×2, Protohedron ×24, T-Creds ×30,000 |
| Mastery 3 | Promotion IV | Mark of Perseverance ×3, Protohedron ×50, T-Creds ×65,000 |

Levels 2 to 9 also take one or two of a plant material, and Mastery levels take rare materials and plants as well. One skill from level 1 to Mastery 3 costs T-Creds ×172,200, Protoprism ×82, Protohedron ×118 and Mark of Perseverance ×6, plus those materials. Version 1.4 added Crimson Spearleaf as a skill mastery material.

What a level changes:

- Damage multipliers rise; Laevatain's battle skill Smouldering Fire goes from 62% to 140% for its base explosion between level 1 and level 12.
- No SP cost, Ultimate Energy cost or ultimate cooldown changes with level. Only combo skill cooldowns do: at level 12 they are 1 s shorter for 23 operators and 2 s shorter for 10; [Arclight](/operators/arclight/)'s stays at 3 s.

## Talents and the Ability Matrix

The Ability Matrix shows an operator's skills, talents and promotion progress. Its Talent Nodes section activates nodes: Attribute Increase (four ranks opened by promotions, also needing 20%, 50%, 100% and 100% Trust), the two talents, two base skills, the three Outfitting nodes and the promotions.

Every operator has two talents, unlocked and upgraded by promotion:

| First talent | Second talent | Operators |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Promotion I, upgraded at II | Promotion II, upgraded at III | 27 operators |
| Promotion I, upgraded at II | Promotion III | [Akekuri](/operators/akekuri/), [Perlica](/operators/perlica/), [Xaihi](/operators/xaihi/) |
| From the start, upgraded at I and III | Promotion II, upgraded at III | [Laevatain](/operators/laevatain/), [Typhoeus](/operators/typhoeus/) |
| From the start, upgraded at II | Promotion I, upgraded at III | [Arcane](/operators/arcane/) |
| From the start, upgraded at I and II | Promotion II | [Ardelia](/operators/ardelia/) |

## Potentials and tokens

Every operator has five potentials, so Potential runs from 0 to 5. Each step takes one Token of that operator, and a Token comes from obtaining the same operator again. Every 240 pulls on one Chartered Headhunting banner also give a Token of its rate-up 6-star operator (see [Headhunting rates and pity](/systems/headhunting/)). Potentials 1, 3 and 5 also unlock a snapshot of the operator to display aboard Dijiang. A 6-star operator at full Potential changes appearance, usually with a glow on the equipment; Version 1.2 added a switch for this Max Potential effect.

## Attributes

Every operator has four attributes. Each raises one stat; on top of that, the operator's main attribute adds a large ATK bonus and the secondary attribute a small one.

| Attribute | Raises | Main attribute of |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | Base HP | 9 operators |
| Agility | Physical Resistance | 10 operators |
| Intellect | Arts Resistance | 7 operators |
| Will | Treatment received | 8 operators |

Levels, gear and potentials add to attributes, as do the Attribute Increase nodes.

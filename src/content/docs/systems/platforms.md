Arknights: Endfield came out on 2026-01-22 at 11:00 on PC, through the GRYPHLINK launcher and the Epic Games Store, and on PlayStation 5, iOS and Android. GeForce NOW followed on 2026-04-03 and Steam with the Version 1.6 update on 2026-10-15. All of them share progress, as long as you play on the same server.

## Platforms and stores

| Platform | Client or store | Available from | Login |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PC (Windows) | GRYPHLINK launcher, from the official website | Release, 2026-01-22 | Gryphline Account |
| PC (Windows) | Epic Games Store | Release, 2026-01-22 | Gryphline Account |
| PlayStation 5 | PlayStation Store | Release, 2026-01-22 | PSN account only |
| iOS and iPadOS | App Store | Release, 2026-01-22 | Gryphline Account |
| Android | Android client | Release, 2026-01-22 | Gryphline Account |
| PC (Windows) | Steam, app 4732690 | After the Version 1.6 update, 2026-10-15 | Steam account linked to a Gryphline Account |
| Cloud | NVIDIA GeForce NOW: its client on Windows PC, Mac and Chromebook, or its web version in Chrome or Edge | 2026-04-03 | Gryphline Account |

- A Gryphline Account can be signed in to through a third-party account such as Google, Apple or Facebook.
- Every platform reaches both servers. GeForce NOW requires a GeForce NOW membership, and its availability depends on country or region.
- The Steam version runs on the same servers as the other platforms, and it is downloaded and updated only through the Steam client, not through GRYPHLINK. Its purchases go through the Steam Overlay.

## Is progress shared between PC, PlayStation 5 and mobile?

Yes, within one server. Account data on the Asia server and on the Americas / Europe server is never shared ([Servers and time](/systems/servers-and-time/)). Purchases made on one platform can be used on the others with the same Gryphline Account.

| Platform | How it joins the shared progress |
| --- | --- |
| PC, Android, iOS, GeForce NOW | Log in with the same Gryphline Account and choose the server of your character |
| Steam | On first launch, Log Into Existing Email Account & Link, then enter the email of the existing Gryphline Account. Register & Link creates a new account instead |
| PlayStation 5 | Link the PSN account to a Gryphline Account; the server follows the region of the PSN account |

## Can I use a controller?

Yes. On PC and mobile devices these controllers are supported; third-party controllers can have compatibility issues.

| Controller | Bluetooth on PC | Bluetooth on mobile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DualSense, DualSense Edge (PS5), DUALSHOCK 4 (PS4) | Adaptive triggers only; no vibration, no controller speaker | Same as PC |
| Xbox Wireless Controller, Xbox Elite Wireless Controller | Full functions | No vibration, no impulse triggers |

- For the Xbox controllers on mobile, the English controller FAQ names impulse triggers and the Traditional Chinese FAQ names adaptive triggers.
- Both connect by USB cable or Bluetooth. A wired connection gives the PlayStation controllers their full functions off PlayStation 5.
- Any controller input on the login screen switches to the controller layout. In play, a button press brings up a prompt to switch; a key press or a touch brings up the prompt to switch back.
- The layout cannot switch on the operator selection and naming screens, in tutorials, in dialogue and story cutscenes, and in some battles and simulations.
- Text is typed on the keyboard on PC and on the system on-screen keyboard on mobile and PlayStation.
- Vibration and adaptive triggers are switched in Settings → Controls, the controller speaker in Settings → Audio.
- Version 1.2 added custom button remapping in Settings → Controller.

## Linking a PSN account to a Gryphline Account

| Situation | What works |
| --- | --- |
| PSN account that has never logged in to the game | At first login, after the user agreement, a prompt links the email of an existing Gryphline Account. Skipping it creates a new Gryphline Account, and the prompt does not come back |
| PSN account that already has progress | Settings → Platform & Account → User Center → Link Email, with an email that is not registered as a Gryphline Account |
| Unlinking | In the Gryphline Account User Center, once per account |
| Limits | One PSN account links to one Gryphline Account, and one Gryphline Account email to one PSN account |

- After linking, PlayStation 5 still takes only the PSN account, and PC and mobile only the Gryphline Account.
- PlayStation trophies for progress made on other platforms unlock at the next login on PlayStation 5.
- On PlayStation 5 the game shows two friend lists: Arknights: Endfield Friends, visible on every platform, and PlayStation Network Friends, visible only on PlayStation 5.

## Steam

| Stage | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| Pioneer Test | A test with limited places before the launch, on the live servers, with purchases and no data wipe |
| Launch | After the Version 1.6 update on 2026-10-15, on both servers. Progress and purchases from the Pioneer Test carried over |

- A Gryphline Account that was used only through Google, Apple or Facebook needs an email linked in the Gryphline Account Center before it can be linked to Steam.
- Steam can be unlinked in Settings → Platform & Account → Account Center, or on the Gryphline Account Center website.

## Updating the game

Version 1.4 consolidated resources and made the mobile client smaller.

| Platform | How to update |
| --- | --- |
| PC, GRYPHLINK | Close the game, restart GRYPHLINK and click Update |
| PC, Steam | Download the new version from the Steam Library |
| Android | Follow the prompt in the game |
| iOS | Update in the App Store |
| PlayStation 5 | On the home screen select the game, press OPTIONS, then Check for Update |

## Known issues with NVIDIA graphics cards

| Since | Issue | Workaround |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-02-07 | Optimization by the NVIDIA App can blur the image and cause stutter | In the NVIDIA App, Graphics → Program Settings → the game → Revert, and turn off automatic optimization of new games; or lower DLSS mode or Graphics Quality in the game |
| 2026-03-17 | GeForce Game Ready drivers 595.79 and 595.71 and NVIDIA Studio driver 595.79 can cause stutter | Tick Launch with DirectX 11 in GRYPHLINK, or reinstall an older driver in the NVIDIA App |

## Launcher and companion tools

- The GRYPHLINK launcher has a real-time data panel (added 2026-07-10): after you log in with your Gryphline Account, View Character Dashboard shows Sanity, Daily Activity Points and Weekly Routine while the game is closed, and switches between the characters of the account.
- Game Tools, opened from Protocol Synchronizer in the game, offer a wiki, a map, the Protocol Terminal and shared team setups.

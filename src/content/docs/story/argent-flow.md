The Operator simply told them to adapt and change between breaths as it was an essential part of Imperial Guards training. While the operators surrounded Argent Flow for advice and tips, be it out of admiration or jest, none of them seemed to realize a crucial fact. Argent Flow neither mentioned the brutal training he endured nor the harsh, unforgiving codes that accompanied his family's secret combat techniques. \

Nevertheless, Argent Flow was a prodigy even when rated alongside esteemed names of past generations. No one in his extended clan moved like a comet with the speed of blazing fire. After his unexpected encounter with his mount, Nuli, the mounted warrior became a thunderbolt of destruction that could not be approached, fought against, nor evaded. However, this also meant that Argent Flow had to train harder and was often given far more dangerous tasks. \

> "But being this good is definitely not easy! They must have been super hard on you!" \

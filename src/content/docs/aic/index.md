## How it works

You place facilities in the open world that mine ore, refine and package it, and power them from a regional grid; the products are materials for gear, tactical items, facilities and trade goods. Valley IV and Wuling each have an AIC with one Protocol Automation-Core (PAC) at its centre, and each region's grid, depot and placement limits are separate.

## Core facilities

| Facility | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Protocol Automation-Core (PAC) | The heart of a region's AIC. It is the only source of the region's power grid, a TP Point, and the place to open Protocol Management (Regional Transfer). Its input and output ports are connected to the region's depot. |
| Core AIC Area | The area around the PAC or a Sub-PAC. Electric Pylons placed here join the grid on their own, transport belts can be placed only here, and here, as near TP Points, the Backpack is connected to the depot. |
| Sub-PAC | A secondary core with the same role in its own Core AIC Area: TP Point, depot ports, automatic power for Electric Pylons in its area. Each outpost has one Sub-PAC, and it works only when connected to the main PAC by power. |
| AIC Steward | Stands next to a Core AIC Area; Regional Stock Bills spent here make the area bigger. |
| AIC Factory Plan | The research tree of AIC technologies, in three phases; Expansion Cores from the main mission open the next phase. |

The PAC and Sub-PACs cannot be placed by hand, only moved, so a blueprint never contains them.

## Regions with an AIC

| Region | Batteries named for it | Outposts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valley IV | LC, SC and HC Valley Battery | 3 |
| Wuling | LC and SC Wuling Battery | 3: Sky King Flats Construction Site, Cardiac Remediation Station (Version 1.2), Xiranflow Cloudseeder Station (Version 1.4) |

Each region has its own depot, and while exploring a region you can reach only that region's depot. Aboard Dijiang you can switch which regional depot you manage. Since Version 1.4, entering Wuling and placing its PAC unlocks a batch of facilities early; they are not usable in Valley IV until the matching technology of the Basic AIC Plan is researched.

## AIC changes by version

| Version | AIC changes |
| --- | --- |
| 1.1 | Wuling AIC II: Hydro Mining Rig, Conduit Inlet and Outlet, Cuprium Ore. Facilities can be constructed anywhere from materials, without returning to the Core AIC Area. Power wires stay connected farther (see [AIC power](/aic/power/)). |
| 1.2 | Wuling AIC III: Acid Resistant Pump Mk II, Conduit Inlet and Outlet Manifold, Purification Unit, Expanded Crucible. New outpost Cardiac Remediation Station. |
| 1.3 | Backup Power. Regional Overview on the Regional Development screen. Multi-Clog Auto-Fix for the Expanded Crucible. Xiranite Pylons and Relays inside the Core AIC Area can connect to Xiranite facilities outside it. |
| 1.4 | Gas AIC mode in Wuling with gas facilities. New outpost Xiranflow Cloudseeder Station. Facilities in the wild can be stashed to the depot from the map. Conduit range up to 300 m. |
| 1.5 | Continue Placing for facilities. The blueprint facility limit shows while box-selecting. |
| 1.6 | Auto-Farmer for the farms of Si's Painting and later Wuling City. A facility can be placed straight from the Backpack or any depot, or built from materials in any depot. |
| 1.7 | [Array-related AIC Mode](/aic/arrays/) in Wuling with the Forge of Equilibrium, the Forges of Luminance and the Rebuilding Supply Port; Exclusion Zone Rebuilding. The Water Treatment Unit became the Waste Treatment Unit. Item and Pipe Control Ports can be placed directly on a facility's port. |

Every version from 1.1 to 1.7 also raised the Regional Development Level cap of Wuling.

## Blueprint sharing rules

Sharing a Blueprint Code lets anyone view, copy and use the design. Placed facilities and blueprints that show discrimination, defamation, obscenity, violence, gambling, hate or other content barred by the Terms of Service can lead to removal of placed items, restricted AIC features or account suspension.

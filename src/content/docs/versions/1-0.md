Version 1.0 is the release of the game on 2026-01-22 at 11:00, the same moment on every platform. It lasted until the [Version 1.1](/versions/1-1/) update on 2026-03-12.

## Release and platforms

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Release | 2026-01-22 11:00 |
| Platforms | PlayStation 5, PC (GRYPHLINK launcher or Epic Games Store), Android, iOS |
| Controllers on PC and mobile | DualSense, DualSense Edge and DUALSHOCK 4; Xbox Wireless Controller and Xbox Elite Wireless Controller |

Platform details are on [Platforms and requirements](/systems/platforms/).

## Launch content

| Kind | At release |
| --- | --- |
| Operators | The launch roster, listed on [Operators](/operators/) |
| 6-star operators in Chartered pools | [Laevatain](/operators/laevatain/), [Gilberta](/operators/gilberta/) and [Yvonne](/operators/yvonne/) as rate-ups; [Ember](/operators/ember/), [Lifeng](/operators/lifeng/), [Ardelia](/operators/ardelia/), [Last Rite](/operators/last-rite/) and [Pogranichnik](/operators/pogranichnik/) in every pool |
| Headhunting | Chartered Headhunting with Urgent Recruitment, new at release: a free 10× attempt after 30 attempts, guaranteed 5-star or higher, not counted toward the banner's guarantee |
| Quota Exchange | AIC Quota buys the current Chartered banner's HH Permits |
| Arsenal | Arsenal Issues paid with Arsenal Tickets, and the Arsenal Exchange, where Arsenal Tickets buy weapons directly |
| AIC | Blueprint sharing: a Blueprint Code lets any other player view, copy and use the blueprint |

Umbral Monument, Delver of the Cryptic, Etchspace Salvage and Weekly Routine were in the game before Version 1.1. Headhunting rules are on [Headhunting rates and pity](/systems/headhunting/), Arsenal rules on [Arsenal and Essences](/systems/arsenal/).

Gilberta and Yvonne were in the pool from release, before their own banners. All three pools have the same eight operators.

## Changes during the version

- An update before 2026-02-04 changed how AIC Factory simulation rewards are claimed and lowered the stats or resistances of some enemies.
- A quick update on 2026-02-05, 08:00 to 08:10, made these changes: enemies at Exploration Level 6 went from level 60 to 55 and at Exploration Level 7 from level 70 to 60, with the same drops as before; Authority Levels 16 to 18 give Ferrium Components and 28 to 30 give Cryston Components, each enough for four pieces of gear; top view mode can switch all selected facilities on or off; enemies near some TP Points, rare gathering sites and mining spots were moved; some Percussive Devices became easier.
- From that quick update, every account that completes Chapter I Process I: Break the Siege receives a Designation Selection Permit ×1 by mail, which picks one 6-star operator of Basic Headhunting.

Version 1.6, Sanctuary of Ink, is the sixth update after the release and the version in which the game came to Steam. The update maintenance ran on 2026-10-15 from 06:00 to 12:00.

## New content

| Kind | Added |
| --- | --- |
| Main missions | Up to Chapter II Process VIII: Paint with Fresh Ink |
| Exploration missions | Miscellanies on Rampage, Post Destination, A Painting Open for Business, Farming Automation |
| Side missions | Back to the Rustics, Unrequited Love, The Story Continues, A Day to Remember, Yuanyuan Innkeeper I to III |
| Area | Wuling - Si's Painting |
| Operators | [Si](/operators/si/) and [Argent Flow](/operators/argent-flow/), both 6-star |
| 6-star weapons | Jade Vessel (Greatsword), Lunar Calling (Sword), Long Heritage (Greatsword), Complexed Industry (Sword) |
| Enemies | Fortune's Stash, Scroll of Tactics, Simmer's Purge, Maned Measure and others |
| AIC facility | Auto-Farmer, for the farm in Si's Painting and later the farming area of Wuling City |
| Mode | Yuanyuan Manor Inn, without Session Tasks |

## System changes

- Combat: the priority order of combo skills can be set on the Team screen, and two more team slots (Team06, Team07) were added.
- Gear Plan: gear setups can be saved, named and switched.
- Sanity Meds with a time limit turn into Sanity when the time runs out; above the Sanity limit they become Sanity Essences if a Sanity Extractor is available, and are destroyed otherwise.
- Cabin Shift Rotation: each cabin holds two operator shifts, switched by hand or automatically.
- AIC: the Regional Development level cap of Wuling was raised, and a facility can be placed directly from the Backpack or any depot.
- Tangtang's ultimate was reworded to say it applies Immobilization; its effect is unchanged.
- Platform: Steam client, with the same account and servers.

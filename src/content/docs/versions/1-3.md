## Update maintenance

Version 1.3 is the third update after the [release](/versions/1-0/). The update maintenance ran that day from 06:00 to 12:00.

## New content

Its new story content is side and exploration missions.

| Kind | Added |
| --- | --- |
| Side missions | Deep Vaulted Steel, A Fistful of Reflections, Danse Macabre |
| Exploration missions | OD Project: Sky King Flats III, Cliffside Riddles of Steel, Trial of Swordmancy |
| Area | Wuling - Sword Vault Dale |
| Operators | [Mi Fu](/operators/mi-fu/) and [Camille](/operators/camille/), both 6-star |
| 6-star weapons | Amaranthine Tassel (Greatsword), Phantom Pain (Greatsword), Blessing of Lustrous Carmine (Polearm), Beacon of Duty (Polearm) |
| Enemies | Rockhowler, Armored Manglerbeast, Craghowler |
| Gear | New pieces for the sets Qingbo, Swordmancer and Grizzled Edge |

## System changes

- AIC: Backup Power keeps every facility of a region running for a while when its power runs out, then needs time to recharge.
- AIC: Regional Overview on the Regional Development screen gathers Stock Redistribution and Depot Node information with map locations; AIC Report has a shortcut on the main screen.
- AIC: the Expanded Crucible clears its cache when all of its formulas are clogged (Multi-Clog Auto-Fix, on by default and saved with blueprints).
- AIC: in Wuling, Xiranite Pylons and Xiranite Relays inside the Core AIC Area connect to Xiranite facilities outside it; the Regional Development level cap of Wuling and the outpost level cap of Sky King Flats Construction Site were raised.
- Gear Artificing Auto-Fill picks several unartificed pieces as material and repeats until success, returning what was not used.
- Weapons: non-6-star weapons can be dismantled in batches in the Valuables Stash; all 5-star weapons can be locked or unlocked at once, and the 5-star copies beyond max potential unlocked at once.
- Essence Wishlist, from Exploration Level 4: a Flawless Essence that matches every stat of a listed weapon is locked and marked Perfect Match.
- Daily Tasks: spending 60 or 120 Sanity now gives 20 Activity Points each instead of 10; Defeat 20 enemies became Defeat 10 enemies; two tasks were added, each worth 20 points.
- Combat: a clearer indicator when switching the locked target, and warnings for attacks from enemies off screen.
- Other: a Return to Dijiang option after visiting a friend, Event Center prompts when key events start, update or near their end, and optional notifications on mobile devices.
- Fixed: operators who lost connection in an Active Blight zone could come back inside it; some operators' sound effects were wrong; Perlica's model could shake in Chapter II Process III: Surging From a Withered Spring on some mobile devices.

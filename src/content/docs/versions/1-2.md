Version 1.2 had one Chartered Headhunting banner. Thunder of Renewal is Tangtang's third Chartered banner, so the next Chartered pool, in [Version 1.3](/versions/1-3/), no longer lists her.

Fest of Brilliance runs on rules of its own, and its attempts and guarantee count are not shared with any other banner. Every attempt there, Urgent Recruitment included, gives Bond Quota ×1. Its totals give Urgent Recruitment (one free 10× attempt) at 30 attempts, Basic HH Permit ×10 once at 60, Shimmering Moment Designation Permit ×1 once at 120 (one free 6-star operator of the four in its pool), and Shimmering Moment Token Supply ×1 for every 240 (a token of one of the four, for an operator you own).

## System changes

- Monthly Pass: daily rewards include Sanity Usage Permit ×4 from this version.
- Sanity Usage Permits: more for sale in Regional Development - Stock Redistribution.
- Combat: an ultimate that lasts a while shows its remaining time at the bottom of the screen, and boss fights in RE-Crisis show a timer.
- Dijiang: Control Nexus Task collects cabin products, sets production and fills clues in one step.
- Recycling Stations at level 4 can be emptied remotely, all at once per area, from the map.
- Gear: a Gear Assembly button on the Operator - Gear screen, and batch assembly of several pieces.
- Headhunting and Arsenal Issue: an option to spend Origeometry without asking for the rest of the day when Oroberyl or Arsenal Tickets run short; a shorter way to skip the headhunting animation.
- AIC: the Regional Development level cap of Wuling was raised; Depot Node delivery jobs can be filtered by region; moving conduit inlets and outlets in the Core AIC Area no longer breaks conduits.
- The story fight against Ereignis can be skipped after some time in combat.
- Controls and graphics: controller buttons can be remapped; frame generation on some Android chips; ray tracing for the Main Pillar Office on some iOS devices.
- Laevatain's ultimate description now says that casting Smouldering Fire or Seethe does not shorten Twilight; the effect is unchanged. Combo skill details now say whether the skill grants Ultimate Energy.
- Fixed: Finisher DMG of Gilberta, [Alesh](/operators/alesh/) and [Fluorite](/operators/fluorite/) could miss some bonuses; Tangtang's ultimate status icon timing did not match the effect; Last Rite's combo skill could cut Tangtang's ultimate short; [Wulfgard](/operators/wulfgard/)'s basic attacks shook the screen when not controlled; Rossi's weapon showed wrong in team builds.

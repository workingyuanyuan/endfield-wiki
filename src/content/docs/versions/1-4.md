## Update maintenance

Version 1.4 is the fourth update after the [release](/versions/1-0/) and a Core Chapter version. The update maintenance ran that day from 06:00 to 12:00.

## New content

From this version a player can start the Chapter II main missions in Wuling right after Chapter I Process II: Remediation Progress, without finishing Chapter I.

| Kind | Added |
| --- | --- |
| Main missions | Up to Chapter II Process VII: Stubbornly Lingers the Setting Sun |
| Areas | Wuling - Yinglung Pass, Wuling - North Wuling Exclusion Zone |
| Operators | [Arcane](/operators/arcane/) and [Liino](/operators/liino/), both 6-star |
| 6-star weapons | Type 42: Solemn Phalanx (Arts Unit), Bedazzling Night Debut (Polearm), Farsight (Sword), Junction Point (Arts Unit), Golden Age (Polearm) |
| Enemies | "Alleikhreos, Chiliarch" (one enemy) and eight kinds of Blightshade, among them YSTF Captain Blightshade |
| AIC | Gas production in Wuling (AIC Plans such as Gas Extraction and Gas Reactor Globe), the gases Inergen and Xiragen, the outpost Xiranflow Cloudseeder Station |
| Gear sets | AIC Fieldwork, Rift Trekker |
| Materials | Talos Cap (promotion), Crimson Spearleaf (skill mastery), Protocolith (weapon tuning) |

## System changes

- Wuling early access: most enemies in Wuling scale with Exploration Level, and placing the Protocol Automation-Core there unlocks a set of AIC facilities that work in Valley IV only after the matching research.
- Combat: the quality and opacity of teammates' skill effects can be set, and targeting has three separate settings (Skill Attack Targeting Priority, Active Target Lock-on, Assisted Target Selection).
- New features: Talosite Exchange (Talosites gathered in Yinglung Pass and North Wuling Exclusion Zone), Transmute (high-tier materials into lower-tier ones in Crafting), Birthday, ID Info Update Card (one free every six months), viewing the 2D artwork of owned operators.
- Operator trial rewards include an Arsenal Defense Crate, a Fully Artificed Level 60 gear set suited to the trial operator.
- AIC: the Regional Development level cap of Wuling was raised; conduit range is measured in a straight line and rose from 200 m to 300 m; facilities outside Automation-Core areas can be stashed from the map; gold gear can switch assembly techniques, and the newest technique cuts stock bill cost by 99%.
- Ornamentals no longer take Backpack or Depot space. Headhunting Records and Issue Records keep 90 days of Chartered Headhunting and Arsenal Issue rewards.
- OMV Dijiang: clue exchange in the Reception Room takes 20 hours instead of 24, and cabins can be upgraded remotely from the Control Nexus.
- Graphics: NVIDIA DLSS 4.5 Super Resolution and AMD FSR 3 on PC, upgraded PSSR on PlayStation 5 Pro.
- Fixed: [Alesh](/operators/alesh/)'s battle skill could not trigger some weapon skill effects after his potential Super Salty Comeback was unlocked.
- Fixed: the screen rotated after some actions with [Snowshine](/operators/snowshine/) as the controlled operator; the camera shook when [Catcher](/operators/catcher/) on the team performed a Final Strike; [Zhuang Fangyi](/operators/zhuang-fangyi/)'s dive attack had no vibration on mobile; Camille's wings displayed wrongly in screen transitions.
- Mi Fu's combo skill sound effects were improved.

## System changes

- AIC: facilities can be built anywhere on the map from carried materials, without returning to the Core AIC Area.
- AIC: shared facilities left by others can be removed from the map screen, and shared ziplines look different from your own; a mini-map shows while mounted on a zipline before moving off.
- AIC: wires stay connected farther, 100 m instead of 90 m for Relay Towers and 50 m instead of 40 m for Electric Pylons; the connection distance is unchanged.
- AIC: top view mode shows a larger area; on PC, Esc can be set to leave it and several selected facilities move together while the left mouse button is held; controller mode has a Move option.
- Regional Development: the caps of the Wuling region, Stock Redistribution, Depot Node, Environment Monitoring and the Sky King Flats Construction Site outpost were raised. At Stock Redistribution level 4 in Valley IV - Origin Lodespring, the Valley Multi-Line Engraving Kit gives an essence with three chosen stats.
- Protocol Spaces of Operator Progression, Weapon Progression, RE-Crisis and Crisis Drills start the team at full HP and full Ultimate Energy, apart from its status outside.
- Rare plants, rare minerals and some Protocol Space materials left on the ground go to the Mail Cache.
- New pieces were added to ten Gear Sets, among them Type 50 Yinglung, Swordmancer and Redeemer. All operators got voice lines when switched in and facial reactions when hit.
- Settings: Camera Distance under Controls and Display Mode under Performance & Graphics.
- Yvonne's ultimate was reworded; its effect is unchanged. The Chinese voice actor credit of [Antal](/operators/antal/) was updated. The 4-star weapon Contingent Measure was renamed Prominent Edge.
- Fixed for Laevatain: wrong Melting Flame stack counts when dashing during, or defeating a Heat Infliction enemy with, the extra attack of her enhanced battle skill; her ultimate ending early when a battle skill was cast just before it ended.
- Fixed for other operators: Yvonne could not perform a finisher; Last Rite's combo skill did not freeze; Ember acted wrongly when hit several times during her battle skill and lost her target on the third basic attack; [Da Pan](/operators/da-pan/)'s ultimate and combo skill lost their target; [Avywenna](/operators/avywenna/)'s potential Carrot and Sharp Stick now matches its description, and Doubling Down no longer activates when it should not.
- Platform: during this version the game became playable on NVIDIA GeForce NOW, with progress shared with the other platforms on the same server.

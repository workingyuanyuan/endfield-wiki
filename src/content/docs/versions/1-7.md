## Update maintenance

Version 1.7 is the seventh update after the [release](/versions/1-0/). The update maintenance ran that day from 06:00 to 12:00.

## System changes

- AIC: [array-related AIC Mode](/aic/arrays/) and AIC Plans for Wuling, with their facilities, simulations and system blueprints. The Regional Development level cap of Wuling was raised, and a Sub-PAC can be deployed in Wuling - Old Dam.
- AIC: Exclusion Zone Rebuilding takes rebuilding supplies made in the AIC at the Rebuilding Supply Port; they advance the Rebuilding Phase of the Exclusion Zone and raise Wuling's regional development.
- Essences: the Wuling Multi-Line Engraving Kit gives an Essence with three custom attributes. One can be bought with Wuling Stock Bills once the Stock Redistribution Terminal of Wuling - Yinglung Pass reaches level 4.
- AIC: Item Control Ports and Pipe Control Ports can be placed directly on a facility's belt or pipe port, without a belt or pipe first, and turn to face it.
- Operators: the Ability Matrix screen lists first the materials that an upgrade lacks.
- Controller settings: a new option inverts the Y axis while flying.
- Fixed: [Si](/operators/si/) (Inksoul, Final Stroke damage, the combo skill after [Alesh](/operators/alesh/) defeats a solidified enemy, Artful Intent's recorded damage, the status icon), [Typhoeus](/operators/typhoeus/) (Barrage Arrays of repeated combo skills, Hovering after the battle skill) and [Arcane](/operators/arcane/) (the combo skill in Array Arcana: INT on the Echoes of War stage Beast File: Qingbo Stockade).

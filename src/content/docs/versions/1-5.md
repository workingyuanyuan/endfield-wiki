Version 1.5, Dreamscape of Wind and Snow, is the fifth update after the release. The update maintenance ran on 2026-09-02 from 06:00 to 12:00. It opened two areas of Wuling and brought the first RE-Factor Headhunting and RE-Factor Issue, banner types with their own guarantee rules.

## New content

| Kind | Added |
| --- | --- |
| Side missions | A Winter Dream Fogged Deep in the Woods, Endeavor of a Lifetime, Illumination of Fate |
| Areas | Wuling - Snowy Forest, Wuling - Suiming |
| Operators | [Typhoeus](/operators/typhoeus/) (6-star), [Purrchena](/operators/purrchena/) (5-star) |
| 6-star weapons | Umbra of Frigid Eventide (Arts Unit), Suffering's End (Arts Unit) |
| 5-star weapon | Dessert Moment (Sword) |
| Enemies | Woodcraft Slug, Woodcraft Wanderer, Gloomwald's Rage |
| Gear set | Deep Rampart |
| Training stage | Typhoeus's Archery Range, with first-clear rewards |

Purrchena was obtained free through the event Purry Big Feline! RAWR! and is in no headhunting banner.

6-star operators in each headhunting pool:

| Banner | 6-star operators that can appear |
| --- | --- |
| Winter Hunt | Typhoeus, [Liino](/operators/liino/), [Arcane](/operators/arcane/), [Ember](/operators/ember/), [Lifeng](/operators/lifeng/), [Ardelia](/operators/ardelia/), [Last Rite](/operators/last-rite/), [Pogranichnik](/operators/pogranichnik/) |
| Resplendent Spectrum | Yvonne, Ember, Lifeng, Ardelia, Last Rite, Pogranichnik |

Winter Hunt is the only Chartered Headhunting banner of this version. [Camille](/operators/camille/) was in the last pool of [Version 1.4](/versions/1-4/) and is not in Winter Hunt: a rate-up operator leaves the Chartered pool after three Chartered banners and is not added to Basic Headhunting. See [Headhunting rates and pity](/systems/headhunting/).

Rules of the two RE-Factor banners:

- Resplendent Spectrum: Yvonne is guaranteed within the first 120 attempts. Urgent Recruitment gives a free 10× headhunting at 30, 60 and 90 attempts, and every 240 attempts give Yvonne's Token ×1. Each of these counts carries over to later Resplendent Spectrum banners, and the guarantee and the Urgent Recruitment bonuses apply once across all of them.
- Tag Artist Issue: after three issues without a 6-star weapon the fourth gives one; this count stays in RE-Factor Issue #1. If the first seven issues give no Artzy Tyrannical, the eighth does; this count carries over to later Tag Artist Issue banners and applies once.
- Chartered HH Permit can be used in RE-Factor Headhunting as well as in Chartered Headhunting.

## System changes

- Progression Overview (under Operators): owned, unowned and upcoming operators and weapons with the materials each progression goal needs and the materials held.
- AIC: the Regional Development level cap of Wuling and the level cap of the outpost Xiranflow Cloudseeder Station were raised; Continue Placing keeps the Construction Status open while facilities, ornamentals or plots are placed one after another.
- Story dialogue can hide the UI, also during Auto-Play. Some stage results screens show the clear time.
- Reception Room: Quick Add fills every clue that fits, and Give Away All Duplicates sends spare clues to friends.
- Gifts to an operator with maximum Trust still count for gifting tasks.
- Fixed: Arcane's battle skill in the Array Arcana: INT form could return wrong SP and deal wrong damage after certain inputs.
- Fixed: Liino's battle skill interrupted an enemy's charge an abnormal number of times on its first hit.
- Fixed: since the Homecoming update, tactical items that restore Ultimate Energy could be consumed on entering some stages.
- The descriptions of [Rossi](/operators/rossi/)'s battle skill (Critical Rate boost) and [Fluorite](/operators/fluorite/)'s battle skill (Slow) were reworded; the effects are unchanged.
- Voice lines of some actions of Arcane and Liino, and some voice lines in [Estella](/operators/estella/)'s Files, were improved.

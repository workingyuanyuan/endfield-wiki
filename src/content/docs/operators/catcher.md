Catcher is the youngest yet remarkably mature member of Endfield's Operations Team Z7, drawing on his military training to provide defensive cover and logistical support.

Files, voice lines and chats: [Catcher's file](/story/catcher/).

## How to obtain

Catcher has no rate-up banner. Catcher is in the standard roster, which Basic Headhunting and every Chartered Headhunting pool draw from.

## Recommended weapons

The weapons the game recommends for Catcher, in the game's order. Each weapon has three Essence stats, one per weapon skill; an Essence raises every skill whose stat it carries. The game also recommends, for every operator, weapons that raise the main and secondary attributes, here Strength and Will.

## Skills

| Slot | Name | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic attack | Basic Tactics | Physical combo of up to 4 hits. As the controlled operator, his final strike also deals 22 Stagger. |
| Battle skill | Rigid Interdiction | He raises a shield to block attacks, granting Protection to himself and nearby operators while returning some SP right away. Taking a hit while blocking triggers a retaliation bash that tries to hit the attacker for Physical damage and 1 stack of Vulnerability. Costs 100 SP. |
| Combo skill | Timely Suppression | Trigger: an enemy begins charging a skill, or the controlled operator is hit and drops below 40% HP. He strikes downward for Physical damage and grants shields scaling with DEF to himself and one teammate, prioritizing the controlled operator. Cooldown 35 s, 33 s at the highest skill level. |
| Ultimate | Textbook Assault | He delivers 2 rapid greatsword slashes for Physical damage and Weakness, then finishes with a heavy downward slam that deals massive Physical damage and Knock Down in an area. Costs 80 Ultimate Energy. |

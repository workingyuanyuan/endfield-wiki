Argent Flow is an Imperial Guard of Yan and Captain of Pacified Skies at Hongshan Academy of Sciences who serves under Tianshi Tan and fights alongside Endfield across Talos-II; despite an aloof reputation he readily helps people he meets.

## How to obtain

Argent Flow is the rate-up 6-star operator of The Hearth Fire Beckons the Starry Flow, a Chartered Headhunting banner of [Version 1.6, Sanctuary of Ink](/versions/1-6/). Banners with Argent Flow:

| Banner | Type | Place | Opens | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Hearth Fire Beckons the Starry Flow | Chartered Headhunting | Rate-up | 2026-11-05 12:00 | End of Version 1.6 |

A limited operator stays in the pool of the two Chartered Headhunting banners after its own, without rate-up, then leaves the pool; limited operators are never added to Basic Headhunting.

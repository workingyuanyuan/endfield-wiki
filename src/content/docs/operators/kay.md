## How to obtain

Kay is the rate-up 6-star operator of Anvil, Backpack, Plus A Pot o'Honey, a Chartered Headhunting banner of [Version 1.7, Meeting You in the World o'Shrooms](/versions/1-7/). Banners with Kay:

| Banner | Type | Place | Opens | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anvil, Backpack, Plus A Pot o'Honey | Chartered Headhunting | Rate-up | Version 1.7 update, 2026-11-25 | 2026-12-23 11:59 |

A limited operator stays in the pool of the two Chartered Headhunting banners after its own, without rate-up, then leaves the pool; limited operators are never added to Basic Headhunting.

### Wild Wacky-Whack

> BASIC ATTACK:\
> An attack with up to 5 sequences that deals Physical DMG. As the controlled operator, Final Strike also deals 17 Stagger.
>
> DIVE ATTACK:\
> Basic attack performed in mid-air becomes a dive attack that deals Physical DMG to nearby enemies.
>
> FINISHER:\
> Basic attack performed near a Staggered enemy becomes a finisher attack that deals massive Physical DMG and recovers some SP.

### "Really Heavy Bash"

> Kay leaps into mid-air for a short power-up, salvages all her Weapons, then unleashes a powerful ground bash with her heavy hammer. The bash deals Nature DMG (based on her Hot Edgy Naives stacks), applies Nature Infliction on enemies within the area of effect, and consumes all Hot Edgy Naives stacks. If Kay consumes 3 Hot Edgy Naives stacks, she also forcibly applies Combustion on enemies.\
> If ＞ 3 Hot Edgy Naives stacks are consumed, the effects will be the same as those for 3 stacks.

### "Really Kwik Smash"

> COMBO TRIGGER: While Kay has ≥ 1 Weapon and either Nature Infliction is applied OR Arts Burst DMG is dealt to the enemy.\
> Kay throws a Weapon at the target to deal Nature DMG. Hitting the enemy recovers some SP and also forcibly applies a Nature Burst. The Weapon will then drop at a random location in the battlefield. Kay salvages the said Weapon when the controlled operator touches it.

### "Really Sweet Combi-Bonker!"

> Kay kitbashes a combi-bonker that she swings multiple times before throwing it to cause an explosion that deals Nature DMG and applies Nature Infliction on enemies within the area of effect, recovers some SP, and leaves behind multiple Picnic Snacks. When the controlled operator touches a Picnic Snack and picks it up, Kay grants HP treatment to the said operator and recovers some SP. The attribute of Agility increases the amount of HP treatment.

## Talents

### Bottomless Arsenal

**Default**

> Kay starts with 3 Weapons. When the battle ends, Kay salvages all thrown Weapons.\
> Salvaging a Weapon grants Kay 1 stack of Hot Edgy Naives.

**Promotion I**

> Kay starts with 3 Weapons. When the battle ends, Kay salvages all thrown Weapons.\
> Salvaging a Weapon grants Kay 1 stack of Hot Edgy Naives and grants the entire team Arts DMG Dealt +1%, with Nature DMG Dealt getting another +1%. Duration: 15s. Max stacks for this effect: 3.

**Promotion III**

> Kay starts with 3 Weapons. When the battle ends, Kay salvages all thrown Weapons.\
> Salvaging a Weapon grants Kay 1 stack of Hot Edgy Naives and grants the entire team Arts DMG Dealt +1.5%, with Nature DMG Dealt getting another +1.5%. Duration: 15s. Max stacks for this effect: 3.

### Have a Waffle!

**Promotion II**

> When an enemy is Staggered or triggers a Stagger Node, it drops 1 Picnic Waffle. Effect only triggers once every 30s.\
> Picnic Waffle has the same effects as Picnic Snack.

**Promotion III**

> When an enemy is Staggered or triggers a Stagger Node, it drops 1 Picnic Waffle. Effect only triggers once every 20s.\
> Picnic Waffle has the same effects as Picnic Snack.

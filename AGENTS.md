# Rules for agents maintaining this site

This site is an unofficial wiki for the game Arknights: Endfield (明日方舟：終末地; Hypergryph, published outside mainland China by Gryphline). Its first readers are AI agents; humans come second. The rules in `../AGENTS.md` apply; this file holds what is specific to this site.

State on 2026-11-22: the look was approved on the sample pages; all pages were written, rewritten under the decisions below, then extended with the game's recommended weapons, base skills, Dijiang and recipes by product (60 pages per language); the enemy resistance table followed the same day (61 pages). Japanese and Korean phase 1 (43 pages each) went live the same day; phase 2 (the 18 hand-written pages: versions, seven system pages, three AIC pages) followed the same day, so both languages have all 61 pages and `records/pending.json` is empty. Published on 2026-11-22 at `https://yuanaruru.com/endfield-wiki/` from `github.com/workingyuanyuan/endfield-wiki` (public; deploy by the workflow, which runs `pnpm verify`). The user site (`workingyuanyuan.github.io`, now a plain index generated from `SITES` in its `src/lib/site.mjs`) lists the site on its home pages and `llms.txt`, and its `robots.txt` names this sitemap (its commit 274f3ed).

## Decisions by the owner (2026-11-21)

- Languages: English at the root, Traditional Chinese (`/zh-tw/`), Simplified Chinese (`/zh-cn/`, written by script from `zh-tw`).
- Scope of the first release: about 55 to 60 pages per language. Versions 1.0 to 1.6 (7), one page per operator (34 to 37), a names page, 10 to 12 pages on systems and AIC (headhunting and guarantees, combat, weapons, gear sets, AIC power, logistics and production chains, regional development, platforms and servers). No page per weapon, gear piece, facility, item or enemy.
- How pages are produced: a mix. Records from which a script writes the Markdown for operators, index pages, the names page and lookup tables; hand-written Markdown for version pages and system pages. The scripts are `pipeline/build_pages.py` (operator pages) and `pipeline/build_lists.py` (names, weapons); they read `records/` only.
- How the home page reaches every page: it links index pages (`versions/`, `operators/`, `systems/`, `aic/`), and each index page links every page of its section within its first 5,000 extracted characters. `scripts/check-dist.mjs` fails for a page that neither the home page nor an index page links.
- Sources of numbers: the game data that warfarin.wiki publishes (it states "Current Version v1.6") is accepted for in-game numbers; the question asked was about numbers (recipes, stats, skill multipliers) only. Read only the pages needed, do not crawl (its `robots.txt` carries `ai-train=no, use=reference` and it states no licence), and check samples against the official notices and endfield.wiki.gg. How the game's own text (story, files, voice lines, skill descriptions) is shown is not decided yet: "restate in our own words" was recorded here without having been decided (corrected 2026-11-23; see the root `AGENTS.md`, Research and provenance).
- Story text (decided 2026-11-23): the game's own story text is shown as the game gives it, in each language's edition from the game's text tables (`I18nTextTable_EN`, `_TC`, `_CN`, `_JP`, `_KR`), never rewritten. Reason: the community wiki of the previous game, PRTS (`prts.wiki`), has carried Arknights story text for years. Remove a text when the publisher asks, as for art.
- Service covered: the global service (Asia and Americas / Europe servers). Mainland China is not covered, as on `nte-wiki`.
- Look: taken from the official site (white ground, grey panels, near-black ink, signal yellow `#fffa00`, square corners, small spaced labels, a double slash before section names). No wordmark, image or font of the publisher. Motion (owner decision, 2026-11-22, in place of the earlier "no motion"; motion and favicon approved from demo videos the same day): modelled on the official site and the login screen, measured that day: 0.2–0.3 s responses to the pointer, a flicker-in for the title and section headings, a yellow line that draws once under the header and along each section rule, a wipe for the answer and for tables, and a cross-document view transition that keeps the header and sidebar still. Each plays once; nothing loops; images do not move; all of it sits inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, and a block waits unseen for its entrance only while the script runs (on screen, not in print). The code is the motion section at the end of `src/styles/endfield.css` and the script in `src/components/SiteTitle.astro`. Favicon (`public/favicon.svg`, 2026-11-22): a yellow inverted triangle with a cut on near-black, drawn for this site; the earlier one was copied from the NTE wiki by mistake.
- Official art is used, by the owner's decision, as on `nte-wiki`. The publisher's Fan Content and Streaming Guidelines (notice 4497, 2026-02-03) prohibit "content that directly copies or uses excerpts of the Company's official materials and lacks clear originality"; the decision stands with that text in view. Credits go in the `art` frontmatter field. Remove an image when the publisher asks.
- Who lets a change go live: the owner confirms every time.
- How the site follows its sources (owner, 2026-11-22, as for `nte-wiki`): a weekly scheduled check that keeps one issue. Fable, Astra and Neon were asked (`~/.local/state/delegation-log/docs-sites/20261122-154634-*`); the owner chose where they differed, and `upstream/README.md` gives the reasons. See "When the sources change".
- Address: `https://yuanaruru.com/endfield-wiki/`.

## Decisions by the owner after reading the preview (2026-11-21)

The site works as `nte-wiki` does.

- Short titles, without a subtitle after a colon and without the game's name in front (幹員列表, as 版本列表): the sidebar shows them. Only the home page title names the game. The question words go in the description and the headings.
- No citation phrases in the prose ("official notices say", "Endfield Talos Wiki lists", "the game data shows"). The page notes name the sources. A sentence names a source only where two sources disagree on something a reader acts on, or where one player test (NGA) is the only source; then once per section.
- No disclaimers: nothing about what is unknown, unannounced, or true "as of" a date. State the positive fact or leave it out.
- No sentence about the site or the page: not who runs the site (the header label 非官方 and `llms.txt` say it), not how many pages a section has.
- Time-limited content stays out: events, limited shops and sales, sign-ins, compensation, pre-downloads, protocol passes, collaborations, the next version, the end of the current one. Headhunting banners and Arsenal Issues stay as a dated history on version and operator pages. Operator pages say nothing that depends on the day they are built. `systems/banner-history` was removed for this reason.
- One clock: dates and times are Asia server time (UTC+8), without a label beside each. The home page caveat, `systems/servers-and-time` and `llms.txt` state once how the Americas / Europe server (UTC-5) relates.
- Player research from the NGA forum (board `fid=846`, mainland players) may serve as a source, as on `nte-wiki`: the combat damage formula comes from the guide 「终末地数据机制导论」 (`tid=46094556`). Player questions from its 提问求助 sub-board went into the question list.
- `pipeline/check_prose.py` checks a page for these five kinds of wording; run it with `lint_page.py` on every hand-written page. `pnpm verify` runs it on every page.

## Expansion and strategy (2026-11-22)

Approved expansion plan: audit the five partial answers, recommended weapons and base skills on operator pages, reverse lookups, a Dijiang page, an enemy table pilot. The publisher's own wiki, `wiki.skport.com/endfield`, is the strongest source and the main competitor.

- Measured that day: every URL of the official wiki, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` included, returns the same 4 KB JavaScript shell with one title and description; entries load from an API that answers 401 without a token the page fetches for itself; the home page has no links to entries. A fetch-only agent gets nothing from it. One web search (one engine, one run) returned no page of it. It is edited by players with the official team (edit log, contribution rank), updated within the hour, in eight languages without Simplified Chinese, and an operator entry is deeper than ours (stats per level, every skill level, voice lines, gallery).
- Strategy, agreed by Fable, Astra and Neon (`~/.local/state/delegation-log/docs-sites/20261122-strategy.*`): readability for agents is a head start that ends if the official wiki renders on the server; the lasting advantage is answers that span entries (which weapon for which operator and back, which operator for which room, which facility for which product), the qualifier next to the fact, version history, player-tested mechanics and Simplified Chinese. Do not race it on depth per entry. Use it as a second witness for fields the game generates (recommendations, base skills), read in a browser at a human pace, never through its API. Question words go in descriptions and headings; titles stay short. Search competitors are guide sites and community wikis.
- Decided by the owner on 2026-11-22, where the advisors disagreed: no links from operator pages to official wiki entries, and no line in `llms.txt` about the official wiki needing JavaScript. Publish before the enemy table. Our records are not published as JSON (all three advisors: later or not at all).
- Kay and Mimino have entries marked as previews on the official wiki: next-version content, left out.
- Pages added: `systems/dijiang` and `aic/recipes` (generated by `build_lists.py`); operator pages gained Recommended weapons and Base skills; the weapons list gained Essence stats and Recommended for. `systems/enemies` (generated by `build_lists.py` from `records/enemies.json`): the 90 encyclopedia entries (89 names; Marble Aggelomoirai has two with the same values), class, the five resistances of the base enemy (enemy id = template id) and where each is found. The game shows a resistance as a grade: the grade of `EnemyDamageTakenLevelTable` with the smallest damage-taken scalar not below 1 − resistance / 100, so 0 is D, 20 to 40 C, 50 and 60 B. Five enemies read on the official wiki (YSTF Captain Blightshade, Breaking Gust, Nimbus Razor, Bonekrusher Arsonist, Tunneling Nidwyrm) gave the same grades for all 25 resistances; it lists fewer areas than the game table for two of them, and the page follows the game table. Stage versions of an enemy (ids with a suffix such as `_hdg025`) often have one value for all five elements; the caveat says so. `systems/combat` said resistances come in tiers D 0, C 20 (30 once), B 50; that sentence now gives the grades and the values found.
- Found while building: 19 AIC recipes use items the game describes as made "during a special period" (event items: Chubby Lung, Xiranite gourds, Proto Xiranite, Proto Xiran-Cuprium); they were in the facility counts of `aic/production` and were removed there.

Hand-written pages in Japanese and Korean (phase 2, 2026-11-22): translated from the English page, which stays the source of every fact. The third-party sources found (Japanese: Altema, AppMedia, GameWith, Gamerch; Korean: Inven, DCInside) are guide sites that restate the rules of the notices (read through searches, not crawled), so they gave no new facts; they gave the players' words (天井, 引き継ぎ, すり抜け, ガチャ; 천장, 이월, 픽뚫, 뽑기), which go beside the game's terms in descriptions and headings. The ja-jp and ko-kr editions of every cited notice (same ids, `cache/official/art/`) gave the official names and phrasing; source URLs stay the en-us ones. A table of names in several scripts gains a first column in the page's language. `pipeline/jako_pages.py` builds the writer's sheet, checks a page against its English page and keeps one set of titles and aliases in all languages.

Site code for the two languages (2026-11-22):

- Partial locale. `records/pending.json` (committed) lists, per language folder, the English pages that language does not have yet; every other English page exists in `ja` and `ko`. `pnpm verify` fails for a missing page that the file does not list, for a listed page that exists, and for a link to a listed page. Japanese and Korean index and home pages list only pages that exist in that language. Remove an id from the file in the commit that adds the page.
- Starlight 0.43 builds an English fallback at the Japanese or Korean address of every page that language lacks, and has no option to turn that off. The fallbacks are kept, so the language picker on an English page never leads to a 404, and `src/routeData.ts` marks each `noindex`, gives it no hreflang links and no JSON-LD and leaves it out of the site search; it also drops such pages from the sidebars and drops hreflang links to languages a page lacks. The sitemap (`src/lib/sitemap.mjs`) lists only pages of the content collection, so fallbacks and their alternates stay out; `llms.txt`, bundles, `index.json` and Markdown twins are built from the collection and never had them. `scripts/check-dist.mjs` checks all of this.
- Description limits: English 110, Chinese 70, Japanese 70, Korean 90 characters (`maxDescription` in `LOCALES`).
- Names in the site layer: titles エンドフィールド Wiki and 엔드필드 Wiki; 非公式 / 비공식; sidebar groups from the game's text tables where the game has the word (オペレーター / 오퍼레이터, 集成工業 / 통합 공업, システム / 시스템), バージョン / 버전 otherwise; version 1.6 is 「丹青渡」 / 「단청도」 (ja-jp and ko-kr text of notice 9560).

## Commands

```
pnpm verify      # prose check, build, then the acceptance checks. Run this before you call a change done.
pnpm upstream    # compare the sources with what the pages were written from (upstream/README.md)
pnpm build       # build
pnpm check       # acceptance checks on an existing dist/
python3 -I pipeline/build_pages.py     # operator pages (en, zh-tw, ja, ko) from records/; ja/ko need the ja/ko fields of records/text/
python3 -I pipeline/build_pages.py --test-fill-english   # fills missing ja/ko text from English: test only, never commit those pages
python3 -I pipeline/build_lists.py     # names, weapons, dijiang, recipes, enemies (en, zh-tw, ja, ko) from records/
python3 -I pipeline/names_jako.py      # records/names-jako.json: ja/ko names and game terms from the text tables and ja-jp/ko-kr notices
python3 -I pipeline/check_jako_names.py [page.md ...]           # ja/ko pages: every game name in its ja/ko form, no Chinese-only names
python3 -I pipeline/jako_pages.py sheet|check|aliases ...        # hand-written ja/ko pages: writer's sheet, check against the English page, one set of titles
uv run --with opencc-python-reimplemented scripts/to-zh-cn.py   # write the zh-cn pages from the zh-tw pages
python3 -I pipeline/lint_page.py <page.md> [...]                # check one page without a build
python3 -I pipeline/check_prose.py <page.md> [...]              # citation phrases, disclaimers, time-limited content, a second server's dates, sentences about the site (en, zh, ja, ko); no argument: every page
python3 -I pipeline/check_text.py <sheet.json> records/text/<slug>.json   # check one operator's restated text
pnpm preview     # serve dist/ at http://127.0.0.1:4398/endfield-wiki/ with GitHub Pages style headers
python3 -I pipeline/notice_text.py cache/official/art/en-us-<cid>.json   # an official notice from the cache as text
python3 -I pipeline/extract_official_operators.py <page script .js> cache/official/operators.json
python3 -I pipeline/wf_extract.py cache/third/wf/<lang>-<slug>.html [...]   # a cached game-data page to JSON
python3 -I pipeline/build_records.py   # facts part of records/operators/ from the cached game data
python3 -I pipeline/text_sheet.py <out dir> <slug> [...]   # the sheet a writer restates an operator from
python3 -I pipeline/wf_misc.py         # terms, tutorials, facilities, weapons, gear sets from the cache
python3 -I pipeline/ed_extract.py      # recommended weapons, base skills, Essence stats, recipes, enemies from cache/third/ed/<commit>/
uv run --with opencc-python-reimplemented pipeline/names_voc.py   # names whose Simplified form is not a plain conversion
```

After a change to a record: `names_jako.py` (when names changed), `build_pages.py`, `build_lists.py`, `to-zh-cn.py`, `check_jako_names.py`, then `pnpm verify`. After new game data: fetch the pages again into `cache/third/wf/` (the fetch scripts are kept there), then `wf_extract.py`, `build_records.py`, `names_voc.py`, and restate what changed in `records/text/`.

## When the sources change

Run `pnpm upstream`. What it watches, what each line of its report means and how to update the pages (a new version, a new banner) are in `upstream/README.md`; read it when an update is due.

## Content rules

- Pages live in `src/content/docs/` (English), `src/content/docs/zh-tw/`, `src/content/docs/zh-cn/`, `src/content/docs/ja/` and `src/content/docs/ko/`. Addresses use the official English name in lower case with hyphens: `operators/laevatain`, `operators/last-rite`, `versions/1-6`. A section's index page is `<section>/index.md`.
- Do not edit `zh-cn` pages by hand. Run `scripts/to-zh-cn.py` after every change to a `zh-tw` page. When a `zh-tw` page brings in a new name, compare it with the mainland site and add a pair to `VOC` when the two differ beyond the script (one did: 洛西 / 洛茜, Rossi). A table whose header names both 繁體中文 and 簡體中文 keeps its rows as written. A sentence that quotes a Traditional name needs an entry in `FIXES`.
- Every page has `aliases`: the title and aliases together are the same set of strings in every language in which the page exists (Japanese and Korean titles included), and the Simplified title must be among the aliases of the `zh-tw` page.
- Japanese (`ja/`) and Korean (`ko/`) pages: links start with `/ja/` or `/ko/`; a page listed for that language in `records/pending.json` is named as plain text, never linked. Japanese is written in だ・である, Korean in -다; numbers, dates, times and links are those of the English page. Game names and terms come from the game's own text (`records/names-jako.json`, written by `pipeline/names_jako.py`; the records of `ed_extract.py` carry ja and ko); a term the tables have is never translated by hand. Template sentences are whole sentences per language in `build_pages.py` and `build_lists.py`; Korean particles after a variable go through `josa()`. `records/changes.json` and `obtain.json` carry ja and ko prose. Hand-written ja/ko pages are translated from the English page (players' words beside the game's terms, official names from the ja-jp and ko-kr notices) and checked with `jako_pages.py check`; after a change of a title or an alias run `jako_pages.py aliases <page id>` and `to-zh-cn.py`. Index and home pages list only pages that exist in that language.
- Write links and images root-relative and without the site's sub-path: `/operators/laevatain/`, `/art/operators/laevatain.webp`. In `zh-tw` pages, links start with `/zh-tw/` (in `ja` and `ko` pages with `/ja/` and `/ko/`); image paths do not.
- Quote a frontmatter value that contains a colon followed by a space (`"Arknights: Endfield ..."`).
- Times: Asia server time (UTC+8), written without a label. "Server time" in a notice is UTC+8 on the Asia server and UTC-5 on the Americas / Europe server; a maintenance window is the same six hours on both (06:00 to 12:00 UTC+8 is 17:00 to 23:00 UTC-5 the day before). A banner that closes "before version update and maintenance" ends at "End of Version x"; do not infer a date.
- Version numbers are not in official notices; they come from Endfield Talos Wiki (source in the page notes of `versions/`). Pages give the number with the title.
- Names: English from the en-us notices, Traditional Chinese from the zh-tw notices, Simplified Chinese from the mainland site or the game's Simplified text. Chinese pages use the game's own Chinese names for skills, states and terms.
- Skill and talent effects are restated in our own words, short; names stay as in the game. This was written under the default corrected on 2026-11-23, not an owner decision: until the owner decides how game text is shown, keep the pages as they are and add no new restated game text. No tier lists, team builds or damage rankings.
- Do not write `//` in page text: the skin draws it before section names and the check script treats it as a leak.
- Operator pages and `systems/names`, `systems/weapons` are written by script. Do not edit them: change the record or the script. `records/operators/<slug>.json` holds names and numbers from the game data (rebuilt by `build_records.py`); `records/text/<slug>.json` holds the restated introduction, skills, talents and potentials in English and Traditional Chinese (written by hand or by a delegate, checked with `check_text.py`); `records/banners.json`, `versions.json`, `obtain.json`, `changes.json`, `weapons.json` are hand-kept from the notices and the game data. `records/recommend.json`, `base-skills.json`, `weapon-essence.json`, `recipes.json` and `enemies.json` are written by `ed_extract.py` from the game tables; base-skill effects are stored as a kind and a number, and the scripts word them. The game's own description text is never committed: it stays in `cache/`.
- A skill's own SP cost, Ultimate Energy cost and combo cooldown are added to the table by the script from the record; the restated text does not repeat them.
- Pronouns in restated text: the operator's name, or the pronoun the game text or an official notice itself uses for that operator.
- Story pages (`story/<slug>`, all 34 operators, 2026-11-23; the Endministrator's text is the same for both player characters, four operators have no chat): the game's text of an operator's files, voice lines and chats, written by `pipeline/build_story.py` from `records/story/<slug>.json` (`pipeline/story_extract.py`, from `CharacterTable` and the SNS tables). Every language, Simplified Chinese included, comes from the game's own text table; `scripts/to-zh-cn.py` leaves `story/` alone. Only the game's markup is removed (Japanese ruby keeps its base text; chat stickers are left out and counted). Check 12 of `check-dist.mjs` fails when a line of the record is not in the page unchanged; smart quotes are off for this reason. `check_prose.py` skips the game's text on these pages. Chats tied to a mission (no topic) wait for the mission names. The description gives the counts; the page notes name the source, and no line in the page says the text is the game's.
- Operator pages follow one structure: portrait, Profile, How to obtain, Recommended weapons, Skills, Skill values (the game's value rows at skill level 1 and 12), Talents, Potentials, Base skills, Base stats, Promotion materials, Changes since release. Portraits are the card images of the official operator list (303 by 386 px), converted to WebP in `public/art/operators/<slug>.webp`.

## Skin rules

- The skin is `src/styles/endfield.css`. Heading font: Lexend (SIL OFL 1.1, from `@fontsource-variable/lexend`); Chinese uses system fonts, with Simplified ones on `zh-CN` pages. Japanese and Korean pages take their own system fonts (`--ef-ja`, `--ef-ko`), so kanji never take Chinese glyph forms; Japanese breaks lines with `line-break: strict`, Korean by word (`word-break: keep-all`); small spaced labels are spaced 0.02em instead of 0.06 to 0.08em on those pages.
- In the light theme yellow is a ground colour only (highlight under links, selected row, marks); text accents are ink. In the dark theme yellow carries links.
- Text drawn with CSS `content`: the double slash before `h2`. A pattern for it is in check 5 of `scripts/check-dist.mjs`.
- Three Starlight components are overridden: `SiteTitle`, `MarkdownContent`, `ThemeSelect`.
- The header links the four sections, in the sidebar's order: versions, operators, systems, AIC (`SiteTitle.astro`, from the sidebar groups). The sidebar puts operators second and keeps the group collapsed. The Markdown twin, `llms.txt` and `index.json` are linked from the page notes, not the header. Check 11 of `check-dist.mjs` fails otherwise. Decided 2026-11-22: the header is for players; the format links and their key hints `[M]` `[L]` `[J]` moved out of it.
- Screenshots: `preview/endfield-wiki/` one level up. Headless Chromium needs `FONTCONFIG_FILE` pointing at a config that adds `/mnt/c/Windows/Fonts`.

Findings that pages rely on:

- The official notices never state a version number. Titles and numbers were paired from the version infoboxes of endfield.wiki.gg (1.1 to 1.6) and Game8 (1.6).
- 37 official entries against 34 in the game data: Endministrator has two entries, and Kay and Mimino appear in no notice up to 2026-11-21.
- The rate-up 6-star operator of a Chartered Headhunting banner is in the pool for three Chartered banners, its own included, then leaves and is not added to Basic Headhunting (every Chartered notice; confirmed on the pools of Laevatain and Liino).
- Official Traditional Chinese writes the 1.1 title with a comma (新潮起，故淵離); endfield.wiki.gg omits it.
- Official notices list only the 6-star operators of a pool. That an operator is in the standard roster, or is a starting operator, comes from the infoboxes of Endfield Talos Wiki; pages say so.
- Purrchena (5-star, Version 1.5) was given by the event Purry Big Feline! RAWR! and is in no headhunting notice.
- Promotions I to III cost the same for all 34 operators, whatever the rarity; Promotion IV differs in two materials (14 combinations).
- 30 names of skills, talents, potentials and promotion stages differ between the game's Simplified and Traditional Chinese texts beyond the script (for example 精英化I and 精英化·一). `records/names-voc.json` lists them and `scripts/to-zh-cn.py` applies them.
- The names of all 34 operators in the game data match the official operator lists in English, Traditional and Simplified Chinese.
- A skill with two forms (Arcane's Array Arcana: INT and WILL) keeps its text under each form's condition, not in the skill's main description.
- The English and Traditional Chinese texts of one notice can differ: the 1.5 update notes list three wording changes only in English.
